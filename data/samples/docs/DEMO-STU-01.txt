Demo Merit Scholarship guidelines.
Eligibility: applicant must be a student aged 25 years or below. Annual family income must not exceed INR 8,00,000.

Documents required: income certificate and bonafide certificate from the institution.
Step 1: Register on the demo portal. Step 2: Upload the income certificate and bonafide certificate. Step 3: Submit and note the demo application number.
